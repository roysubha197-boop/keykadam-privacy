KeyKadam Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

KeyKadam is an offline typing and data-entry practice application for Windows. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information used by the app

To keep separate learning records on a device, KeyKadam can store a learner name or nickname and practice information such as completed lessons, test attempts, typing speed, accuracy, completion status, and related dates. While a learner is doing an exercise, the app processes the characters they type to show practice feedback. The saved test records contain results and summaries, not a copy of the complete passage typed.

Where information is stored

This information is stored locally in KeyKadam's app data on the Windows device. KeyKadam does not require an account, and the app does not send these learner names or practice records to the publisher or to an online service. The app does not use advertising or analytics services.

Anyone with access to the same Windows user profile or device may be able to access locally stored records. On shared computers, use a nickname or other suitable identifier instead of a learner's full legal name, and sign out of or secure the Windows account when appropriate.

Exports

If a user chooses the app's class-list export feature, KeyKadam creates a CSV file on the device. The exported file can include learner names and summarized practice results. The user is responsible for where that file is saved, who can access it, and deleting it when it is no longer needed. Exporting does not upload the file.

Choice and deletion

Entering a learner name is optional; a guest profile can be used. The app provides controls to reset or delete learner records. Removing an exported CSV file is a separate step. Local data may also be affected by Windows app-data management or uninstall behavior.

Children and classroom use

KeyKadam may be used for typing practice in a learning setting. Teachers, schools, and parents should choose an appropriate identifier for each learner, avoid entering sensitive information, and manage access to shared devices and exported files in line with their policies and applicable law.

Changes to this policy

If KeyKadam's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the revised date will be shown above. Material changes will be reflected in a future app or Store listing update as appropriate.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the support contact published on KeyKadam's Microsoft Store listing.
